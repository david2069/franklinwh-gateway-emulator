# #352 Web UI: an optional map of where the gateways are installed, 2026-11-21

Approach copied from franklinwh-direct-connect-bridge's CloudFront PoP map: Leaflet 1.9.4 vendored
(byte-identical to the bridge's copy, sha256 db49d009...641a), loaded on first use, circle markers with
tooltips, Leaflet objects kept out of Alpine's reactive data. Difference: no online OpenStreetMap
tiles; the base is a shipped Natural Earth 1:110m outline (land, country borders, state/province
lines; 187 kB), so it works offline. Shown inline above the cards (the bridge opens a dialog).
