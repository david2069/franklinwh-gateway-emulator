## Found while building it
- The docked column's `main.layout-right .col-side` (sticky) outranked `.actions.drawer`: the drawer
  stayed in the column. The drawer rules start with `main` (break check 2).
- Focus didn't reach ×: x-show reveals elements in its own later timeout, so the buttons use
  `:hidden`, and the focus retries briefly for the drawer layout, where the panel itself uses x-show.
- On a phone Leaflet's layers (z-index 400-1000) drew over the drawer; `.fleet-map` now isolates
  its stacking context.
